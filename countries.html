Cuisines around the world

Every cuisine reflects its country's history, ingredients and traditions.

KZ

Kazakhstan

Traditional Kazakh cuisine reflects nomadic culture and hospitality.

  • Beshbarmak
  • Baursak
  • Kazy

Meat, dairy and bread are at the heart of Kazakh food. Beshbarmak is traditionally served to guests, and tea with baursak is part of every celebration.

IT

Italy

Italian cuisine is known for fresh ingredients and simple recipes.

  • Pizza
  • Pasta
  • Risotto

Every region of Italy has its own specialties: pizza comes from Naples, while risotto is popular in the north. Italian cooks use a few fresh ingredients and cook them well.

JP

Japan

Japanese cuisine values balance, presentation and seasonal ingredients.

  • Sushi
  • Ramen
  • Tempura

Rice and fish are the base of many Japanese dishes. Sushi, ramen and tempura show how simple ingredients and careful technique work together.

MX

Mexico

Mexican cuisine combines tortillas, vegetables, beans and spices.

  • Tacos
  • Quesadillas
  • Enchiladas

Corn is the foundation of Mexican food. Tortillas, salsas and chili peppers appear in many dishes, and each region has its own flavours.

IN

India

Indian cuisine includes spices and many regional traditions.

  • Biryani
  • Dal
  • Naan

Cooking changes from north to south: the north is known for naan and creamy curries, the south for rice and coconut. Spices like turmeric, cumin and cardamom are used everywhere.

KR

South Korea

Korean cuisine combines rice, vegetables and fermented foods.

  • Kimchi
  • Bibimbap
  • Tteokbokki

A Korean meal usually comes with several small side dishes called banchan. Kimchi, made from fermented vegetables, is served almost every day.
